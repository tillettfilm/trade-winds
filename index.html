How it comes together.

The brand
Steve at Clever Little Design is doing the rebrand. Every frame is shot to his direction: place over polish, real moments, nothing facing the camera. The brand runs the same way through everything.
The Table

Guests are told before the night that the first half hour is being filmed. No audio is recorded at the table. Releases are signed on arrival. Anyone who'd rather not be on camera sits out of shot or books the next Table instead.

Then the camera leaves. Chatham House starts when the camera leaves.

Member interviews cover their own experience only, never what anyone else said.

Member stories
The full member stories wait for the founding members, because a member who's joined saying why is the strongest proof there is.
